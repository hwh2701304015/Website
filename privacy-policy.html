Privacy Policy

This policy explains how AmazeMend handles website, support, licensing, and purchase-related information.

Last updated: 2026-10-10

This Privacy Policy describes how AmazeMend, an independent software brand operated from Shenzhen, Guangdong, China ("AmazeMend", "we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, and protects personal information when you visit this website, download our software, contact support, or purchase a license.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact and support information: your email address and any order details, messages, files, or other information you choose to send when requesting support.
  • Purchase information: order ID, selected plan, purchase email, transaction status, renewal status, and refund status received from our payment provider.
  • License and device information: license key, device fingerprint, operating system, computer name, application version, activation time, IP address, and license status used to activate and protect your license.
  • Website information: standard request and security data such as IP address, browser type, requested page, and time of access that may be processed by our hosting provider.

2. Your Video Files

AmazeMend Video Repair processes video files locally on your computer. Your videos are not uploaded to AmazeMend as part of the repair process. If you voluntarily send a sample file to support, we use it only to investigate your request and delete it when it is no longer needed for that purpose.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the website and software.
  • To process purchases and manage license activation.
  • To respond to customer support requests.
  • To send transactional notices, product updates, or service-related communications.

4. Payment Processing

Purchases may be processed by Paddle as merchant of record. Paddle may collect and process payment details, billing information, tax information, fraud prevention signals, and transaction records according to its own privacy notice. AmazeMend receives purchase and license-related information such as order ID, product plan, customer email, license status, and renewal or refund status so we can provide software access and support.

5. Cookies & Analytics

We use only cookies or similar technologies needed to operate the website, open secure checkout, prevent fraud, and provide license-related services. AmazeMend does not currently use advertising cookies or third-party behavioral analytics on this website. Paddle may use its own cookies when you interact with checkout, subject to its privacy notice.

6. Data Retention

We retain purchase, tax, and transaction records for as long as required by applicable law. License and activation records are retained while a license remains active and for a reasonable period afterward for fraud prevention, dispute handling, and support. Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed to resolve the request and meet legal obligations.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction or objection to the processing of your personal information, and to request a portable copy. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. To submit a request, contact support@amazemend.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

9. International Transfers

We are based in China and use service providers that may process information in other countries or regions. When personal information is transferred across borders, we use appropriate contractual, technical, and organizational safeguards and require our service providers to protect it in accordance with applicable data protection laws. The privacy laws in those locations may differ from the laws where you live.

10. Children's Privacy

AmazeMend is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or legal obligations change. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

12. Contact

AmazeMend is an independent software brand operated from Shenzhen, Guangdong, China. For privacy questions, contact support@amazemend.com.
